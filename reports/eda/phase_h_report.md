## Scope (what Phase H is, and deliberately is not)

Phase H is the ONE final sealed-holdout evaluation of the frozen Phase F/G
design -- `holdout` is reserved for this phase alone (`configs/split.yaml`'s
own docstring), and this is the one, single, authorized place in this
entire project `holdout` rows are ever loaded from disk. Explicitly:

- **`sentinelpay.target_history`, `sentinelpay.model_features`, and
  `sentinelpay.model_evaluation` are all consumed COMPLETELY UNMODIFIED.**
  No new causal-history logic beyond one small, explicitly-scoped
  extension: holdout's Phase F target-history recipient pool.
- **Protocol: Option A, online causal continuation** (explicitly settled
  before implementation) -- a holdout row may use strictly-earlier holdout
  labels sharing its `payment_proxy_key`, in addition to `train`+
  `validation`; future and same-`TransactionDT` holdout labels are excluded,
  exactly like every other recipient. `embargo_1`/`embargo_2` remain
  excluded from every recipient's Phase F source pool, always. Phase D's
  non-target history remains unrestricted across all five partitions,
  unchanged.
- **Only `B2`/`F1`/`F2` are refit (on `train` only) and evaluated on
  holdout.** `B0`/`B1` are NOT touched -- their numbers below are Phase G's
  own validation-partition results, quoted verbatim, never recomputed.
- **The same four Phase G graduation gates, pre-declared, reused verbatim**
  -- Phase H confirms whether the graduated design generalizes; it does not
  re-derive new thresholds.
- **One-shot**: this evaluation runs at most once. A code change to any of
  the three frozen modules after this report exists permanently invalidates
  it for this design.
